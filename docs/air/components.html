Quiet doesn’t mean
without character.

Air is built from open space, softly lit edges and deliberate alignment. The same details carry from the first page to the deepest control.

01 · Space

Group by proximity.

Related controls sit together. Larger gaps signal a new task. A border is useful only when it explains a real boundary.

02 · Material

Solid, with a little depth.

Matte surfaces, quiet boundaries, and shadows only where they explain depth. Dark mode is graphite with clear separation.

03 · Signature

A precise touch of blue.

The family’s blue becomes a fine selection marker and a quiet light horizon. Content never has to compete with it.

Actions, with hierarchy.

One leading action. The rest step back. Stable alignment, optical icon spacing and a small, tactile press.

Fields, with clarity.

One quiet contour. Persistent labels and nearby help. Focus replaces the contour with blue, without a second line or a change in layout.

Saved to your chosen location.

Start with page 1 or later.

Choices, with precision.

Neutral selection surfaces. Blue for focus and the active indicator. Visual colour data keeps its real values.

Feedback, without noise.

Short, useful messages. A status colour supports the words. Recovery is available where the problem occurs.

A little lift.
Exactly when it matters.

Playfulness comes from response and timing. The interface acknowledges your actions, then gets out of the way.

Finishing should feel good.

A brief, soft settle confirms completion. No confetti, looping ornaments or moving text. Try it again—the interaction restarts cleanly without stacking effects.
