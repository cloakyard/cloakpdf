Quiet doesn’t mean
without character.

Air is built from open space, softly lit edges and deliberate alignment. The same details carry from the first page to the deepest control.

01 · Space

Group by proximity.

Related controls sit together. Larger gaps signal a new task. A border is useful only when it explains a real boundary.

02 · Material

Solid, with a little depth.

Matte surfaces, quiet boundaries, and shadows only where they explain depth. Dark mode is graphite with clear separation.

03 · Signature

A precise touch of blue.

The family’s blue becomes a fine selection marker and a quiet light horizon. Content never has to compete with it.

A little lift.
Exactly when it matters.

Playfulness comes from response and timing. The interface acknowledges your actions, then gets out of the way.

Finishing should feel good.

A clear lift and soft landing confirm completion. No confetti, looping ornaments or moving text. Try it again—the motion continues from its current position without stacking effects.

Editing · immediateTyping, shortcuts and tool changes never wait.

Reduced motion preserves feedback without spatial movement. Theme changes are immediate. Air’s two planes catch a soft light on arrival and respond to a mouse. The landing artwork rests when idle and releases its graphics resources when hidden or offscreen. Review the motion and its still alternative before the design is locked.

Light study · rendering notes

Custom Air composition, locally bundled with Shaders 4.0.5. No hosted presets, media, telemetry or continuous loop. Reduced motion, unavailable graphics and constrained devices use the still artwork. Pointer response is for a mouse; touch keeps the brief arrival.
